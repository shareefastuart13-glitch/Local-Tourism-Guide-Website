Accommodation

Choose a comfortable base, from central hotels to peaceful hideaways in nature.

Where to stay

Four ways to stay

The area offers a range of accommodation styles. Use the overview below to decide which type suits your plans, then contact us if you would like suggestions.

Hotel

Town Centre Hotels

Location: Central business district, within walking distance of shops, restaurants and the bus terminal.

Key facilities

  • Air-conditioned rooms with private bathrooms
  • On-site restaurant and room service
  • Swimming pool and fitness room
  • 24-hour reception and free Wi-Fi
  • Airport transfers on request

Best for: business travellers and visitors who want convenience.

Make an Enquiry about hotels

Guesthouse

Family-Run Guesthouses

Location: Quiet residential streets close to the old town and riverside.

Key facilities

  • Homely rooms with fans or air conditioning
  • Home-cooked breakfast included at many properties
  • Shared lounge and garden or veranda
  • Local advice from friendly hosts
  • Free Wi-Fi in common areas

Best for: couples and families seeking a personal, local experience.

Make an Enquiry about guesthouses

Eco-lodge

Hillside and Forest Eco-Lodges

Location: Rural hills and forest edges, around 30 to 60 minutes from town by road.

Key facilities

  • Cabins built with natural and local materials
  • Solar power and rainwater systems
  • Guided nature walks and birdwatching
  • Meals made with locally grown produce
  • Limited signal, ideal for switching off

Best for: nature lovers and travellers seeking peace and quiet.

Make an Enquiry about eco-lodges

Budget stay

Hostels and Budget Rooms

Location: Near the market and main transport routes, convenient for getting around.

Key facilities

  • Private rooms and shared dormitories
  • Shared kitchen and lounge
  • Secure lockers and luggage storage
  • Bicycle hire at some properties
  • Notice boards for tours and group trips

Best for: solo travellers, students and longer stays on a budget.

Please note

This guide describes general types of accommodation and does not take bookings. Facilities vary by property. Please confirm prices, availability, cancellation policies and booking terms directly with the accommodation provider before you travel.
