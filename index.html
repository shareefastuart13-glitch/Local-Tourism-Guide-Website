Why visit

A destination with something for everyone

Whether you come for a weekend or stay for a week, the area offers a rich mix of landscapes, history and flavour.

Natural beauty

Follow shaded trails to tumbling waterfalls, paddle calm rivers and unwind on quiet sandy shores. Green spaces are never far away.

Culture and heritage

Walk historic streets, explore museums and galleries, and learn the stories that shaped the community through its buildings and traditions.

Food and local experiences

Taste fresh produce at the market, try street food at sunset and join hands-on workshops led by local makers and cooks.

Travel smart

A little preparation goes a long way

Our travel tips cover the practical details that make a trip smoother, safer and more enjoyable.

Carry water

Warm days and outdoor walks call for regular breaks and plenty of fluids.

Start early

Popular sites are cooler and quieter in the morning.

Keep small change

Markets, buses and street food stalls often prefer cash.
