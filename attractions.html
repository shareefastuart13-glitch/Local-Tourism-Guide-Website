Attractions

Waterfalls, markets, museums and more. Find the experiences that match your interests.

Things to do

Explore by interest

Use the buttons below to filter attractions by category. Each listing includes a short visitor tip to help you make the most of your time. Opening times can change by season, so check locally before you set out.

Nature

Cascade Falls Trail

An easy-to-moderate forest path leading to a dramatic waterfall. Benches and a viewing deck make it a pleasant stop for all ages.

Visitor tip: Wear shoes with good grip, as rocks near the water can be slippery.

Nature

Riverside Botanical Gardens

Landscaped lawns, flowering plants and a glasshouse of tropical species. A peaceful place for a picnic or slow afternoon stroll.

Visitor tip: Visit in the late afternoon for softer light and cooler temperatures.

Culture

Living Culture Museum

Exhibits on music, crafts, festivals and everyday life tell the story of the many communities who call the area home.

Visitor tip: Ask at the front desk about guided talks, which often run at set times.

Culture

Community Arts Centre

Rotating exhibitions by local artists, a small craft shop and regular evening performances, from drumming to spoken word.

Visitor tip: Check the noticeboard by the entrance for weekend workshops.

Heritage

Old Town Walking Route

A self-guided loop past painted wooden townhouses, historic churches and quiet squares. Information boards explain key buildings.

Visitor tip: Allow around two hours and carry a hat, as some streets have little shade.

Heritage

Harbour Fort and Lighthouse

Old defensive walls with sweeping sea views and a landmark lighthouse. A favourite spot for photographs and sea breezes.

Visitor tip: Sunset is especially beautiful here, so plan an evening visit.

Food

Central Market

Rows of stalls selling fresh fruit, vegetables, spices, snacks and handmade souvenirs. A colourful introduction to local life.

Visitor tip: Arrive early for the best choice and bring small notes for purchases.

Food

Riverside Food Street

As evening falls, food carts serve grilled dishes, savoury pastries, fresh juices and sweet treats along the waterfront.

Visitor tip: Choose busy stalls where food is cooked fresh in front of you.

Recreation

River Kayaking

Paddle calm stretches of river with experienced local operators. Suitable for beginners, with guided and self-led options.

Visitor tip: Always wear the life jacket provided and apply waterproof sunscreen.

Recreation

Sandy Bay Beach

A wide sandy beach for swimming, beach games and relaxing in the shade of palm trees. Small cafés operate nearby.

Visitor tip: Swim only in marked areas and follow any posted safety flags.
