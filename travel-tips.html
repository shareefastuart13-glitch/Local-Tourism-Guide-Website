Practical advice to help you travel comfortably, safely and respectfully.

Travel advice by topic

Getting around

  • Minibuses and shared taxis serve most main routes. Confirm the fare before you board.
  • Use registered taxis or ride services, especially at night.
  • The old town and riverside are best explored on foot.
  • Allow extra time for travel to rural areas, as some roads are narrow or unpaved.

Weather and what to pack

  • Expect warm days with occasional heavy showers. Check the forecast each morning.
  • Pack light, breathable clothing, a hat and sunglasses.
  • Bring a compact umbrella or rain jacket for sudden downpours.
  • Comfortable walking shoes and insect repellent are essential for outdoor trips.

Safety and wellbeing

  • Keep valuables out of sight and use your accommodation safe where available.
  • Drink plenty of bottled or filtered water and rest during the hottest hours.
  • Use sunscreen and reapply after swimming.
  • Save local emergency numbers and your accommodation address on your phone.
  • Travel insurance that covers medical care is strongly recommended.

Money and communication

  • Cards are accepted in many hotels and larger shops, but carry cash for markets and buses.
  • Use ATMs inside banks or shopping centres where possible.
  • A local SIM card or eSIM is usually the easiest way to stay connected.
  • Download offline maps before visiting areas with limited signal.

Respecting local culture

  • A friendly greeting goes a long way when entering shops or asking for help.
  • Dress modestly when visiting places of worship.
  • Ask permission before photographing people or private homes.
  • Bargaining may be fine at markets, but always stay polite and fair.

Sustainable travel

  • Carry a reusable water bottle and shopping bag.
  • Stay on marked trails and never feed or disturb wildlife.
  • Choose locally owned guides, cafés and craft sellers.
  • Take your litter with you, especially from beaches and riverbanks.
